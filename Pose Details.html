Virabhadrasana II

How to move

Step wide. Turn the front toes forward and bend the front knee over the ankle. Reach both arms long and gaze over the front fingertips.

Breathing

Inhale to create length through the spine; exhale to ground through the legs.

Benefits

Builds lower-body endurance and shoulder stability.

Common mistakes

Letting the front knee collapse inward or shortening the stance.

Contraindications

Modify for acute knee, hip, shoulder, or balance concerns. Stop for pain.
